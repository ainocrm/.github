<h1 align="center">AinoCRM</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Focus on customers. Aino does the rest.</strong></p>

AinoCRM is an AI-native CRM for B2B sales teams that you talk to instead of fill out. Reps describe what happened in a chat or forward an email; AinoCRM works out which account, contact and deal they mean (creating them if new), updates the pipeline, scores each deal against the company's own sales playbook, and tells reps what to do next.

Aino keeps the pipeline current, runs the routine work and gives every rep, leader and executive the same live picture of where sales stands.
